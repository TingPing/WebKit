An ImageBitmap that is transferred to a worker can be transferred back, also when a pattern made the transfer copy it.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


From a canvas, not referenced by a pattern:
PASS result.width is 30
PASS result.bitmap is non-null.
PASS result.bitmap.width is 30
PASS pixel is "0,128,0,255"

From a canvas, referenced by a pattern:
PASS result.width is 30
PASS result.bitmap is non-null.
PASS result.bitmap.width is 30
PASS pixel is "0,128,0,255"

From a blob, not referenced by a pattern:
PASS result.width is 30
PASS result.bitmap is non-null.
PASS result.bitmap.width is 30
PASS pixel is "0,128,0,255"

From a blob, referenced by a pattern:
PASS result.width is 30
PASS result.bitmap is non-null.
PASS result.bitmap.width is 30
PASS pixel is "0,128,0,255"

PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
